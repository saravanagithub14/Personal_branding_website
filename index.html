About

Researcher turned AI builder

I didn't start in tech. I started in a biology lab, working on stem cells, vaccines and 3D bioprinted skin. I taught myself AI from scratch, built my own projects, and then started teaching it.

I spent about seven years in research, at IISER Thiruvananthapuram, ICAR's Central Institute of Brackishwater Aquaculture in Chennai, and Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute (SCTIMST). In 2023 I moved into data science, then into generative AI. I've been teaching and mentoring since February 2024.
