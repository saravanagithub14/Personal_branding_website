If you've ever thought…

Thoughts I hear every day, and my answers

Learning AI comes with a lot of questions. That's normal, and every one of them has an answer. Here are the ones I hear most.

The seven I hear most

  1. 01

    “There are so many AI tools and courses. Where do I even start?”

    Most common among: Beginners and busy professionals

    My answerWith a clear roadmap made for you: what to learn first, what to skip for now, and a first project you can finish.

  2. 02

    “I tried ChatGPT, but the answers were just okay.”

    Most common among: Almost everyone

    My answerGood results come from good prompts. I'll show you simple prompt frameworks that turn average answers into useful ones, and how to reuse them.

  3. 03

    “I've watched so many videos, but I haven't built anything.”

    Most common among: Learners stuck watching tutorials

    My answerEvery session ends with something you've built. We learn by doing, and I check in so you keep going.

  4. 04

    “I'm juggling five AI tools, and none of them talk to each other.”

    Most common among: Power users and business owners

    My answerWe design one simple workflow around your real work, connect the pieces that matter, and drop the tools you don't need.

  5. 05

    “I can get AI to do it, but I'm not sure I really understand it.”

    Most common among: Students and early-career learners

    My answerWe build the fundamentals alongside the shortcuts: how language models work, in plain terms, so you use AI with judgement instead of copy and paste.

  6. 06

    “How does AI apply to my field, and is it worth it?”

    Most common among: Professionals and entrepreneurs

    My answerWe start from the tasks in your field, like reports, documents, customer replies and data, and apply AI there first, so you can see what it's worth to you.

  7. 07

    “Everything moves so fast. Am I already behind?”

    Most common among: Everyone

    My answerYou're not. You don't need to chase every release. A few solid habits and one workflow that works will carry you further than any trend.

Every other thought, answered

Starting out

  • “I need someone to tell me what to do in my situation, not another generic course.”

    That's what mentoring is for. We look at where you are and where you want to be, and plan from there.

  • “I'm worried I'll be rushed or talked down to.”

    Never. I was a beginner too. We go one idea at a time, at your pace.

  • “My questions feel too basic to ask.”

    There's no such thing. One-on-one sessions mean you can ask anything, with no audience.

  • “My kids use AI better than I do.”

    That's easy to change. In plain language, and in your own language, you'll be using it with confidence.

  • “I start strong, then life gets in the way.”

    Regular check-ins with a real person keep you moving.

  • “I've already spent money on courses that didn't help.”

    Before you commit to anything, I'll send you a clear plan for your goal, so you know exactly what you're getting.

  • “How do I know who's a real expert?”

    Look at what they've built. I don't teach what I've only read about: you can see my projects and publications.

Careers and study

  • “Will AI take my job?”

    AI changes how work gets done, and people who can use it well are in a strong position. Let's make sure that's you.

  • “My degree taught me theory. Employers want AI skills.”

    We add the practical side: building with generative AI and agentic AI, on real projects.

  • “I don't have any projects to show.”

    You will. What we build together becomes projects you can put on your resume and talk about.

  • “I've applied everywhere and heard nothing back.”

    I've mentored students through job placements. We work on projects that stand out, and a clear story about what you can do.

  • “Is using AI for my assignments cheating?”

    It depends on how you use it. In a college workshop, students and faculty can work out together where AI supports learning and where it replaces it.

At work

  • “My boss says 'use AI', but nobody has shown us how.”

    That's what corporate AI training is for. We start from your team's real tasks and build AI into them.

  • “I don't have time for a long course.”

    Sessions stay focused on your real work, and you leave with something you've built and can use.

  • “Replies, content, invoices and follow-ups eat my whole day.”

    Simple automations can take a lot of that off your plate. We start with the tasks that cost you the most time.

  • “I've been asked for an AI plan, and I don't know where to begin.”

    Tell me your team's size and the work they do, and I'll propose a plan. You'll be able to judge tools and vendors by what they do for your work.

Trust and safety

  • “Is it safe to paste company or client data into AI tools?”

    You'll leave with clear rules for using AI safely at work: what's fine to share, and what to keep out.

  • “It gave me a wrong answer once. How can I trust it?”

    You'll learn why AI gets things wrong, how to check its answers, and how to build a chatbot that answers from your own documents.

  • “How do I know if a call or a video is real?”

    Knowing how AI voices and videos are made takes away most of the worry. We'll go through what to double-check before you trust one.

Whatever your thought is, there's an answer. Tell me yours, and I'll tell you where to start.

About

Researcher turned AI builder

I didn't start in tech. I started in a biology lab, working on stem cells, vaccines and 3D bioprinted skin. I taught myself AI from scratch, built my own projects, and then started teaching it.

I spent about seven years in research, at IISER Thiruvananthapuram, ICAR's Central Institute of Brackishwater Aquaculture in Chennai, and Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute (SCTIMST). In 2023 I moved into data science, then into generative AI. I've been teaching and mentoring since February 2024.
