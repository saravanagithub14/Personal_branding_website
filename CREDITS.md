## Pixelarticons

The site's icons are [Pixelarticons](https://pixelarticons.com) by Gerrit Halfmann, used under the MIT licence. Icon data comes from the npm package `@iconify-json/pixelarticons` (version 1.2.9) and is stored in `assets/icons/icons.json`. Four icons (brain, dna, graduation-cap, microscope) are custom pixel icons drawn in the same style for Saravana Perumal R's portfolio.
