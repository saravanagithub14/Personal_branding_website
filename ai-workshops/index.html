FAQ

Questions colleges ask

Do you run AI workshops for colleges and schools?

Yes. As an AI workshop trainer, I run half-day and full-day workshops for students and faculty, in person in Kerala or online anywhere in India.

Do I need coding or a technical background to join your AI training?

No. Most of my learners are not technical. We start with how AI works in simple terms and with tools you can use right away. Coding is added only if your goal needs it.

What is the difference between generative AI and agentic AI training?

Generative AI training covers tools that create text, images and answers from a prompt, like ChatGPT. Agentic AI training goes a step further: AI that plans, uses tools and completes multi-step tasks on its own. I teach both, starting with generative AI.

What are your fees for AI training?

Fees depend on the audience, group size, number of days, and whether the session is online or in person. Message me with those details and I'll send you a quote.
