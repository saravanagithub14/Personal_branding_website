Six ways to work together

Training and mentoring options

  • Corporate AI training for your team

    I work as an AI corporate trainer for companies that want their people using AI properly, not just trying ChatGPT once. We start with the tasks your team actually does, then build AI into those: reports, emails, documents, customer replies, data. Your team leaves with working prompts, simple automations and clear rules for using AI safely at work. No technical background needed.

    More on corporate AI training

    Photo: corporate training session3:2

  • AI workshops for colleges and communities

    As an AI workshop trainer, I run half-day and full-day sessions for students, faculty and mixed groups. Every workshop is hands-on: participants build something small and real before they leave. Online or in person, in English or Malayalam, Tamil and Hindi.

    More on AI workshops

    Photo: college workshop3:2

  • Generative AI training

    I'm a generative AI trainer for beginners and working professionals across India. You'll learn how large language models work in plain terms, how to write prompts that give useful answers, and how to build your own chatbot that answers from your own documents.

    Ask about generative AI training

    Photo or screenshot: a chatbot built in class3:2

  • Agentic AI training

    As an agentic AI trainer, I teach how AI agents plan, use tools, search, and finish multi-step tasks. This is the newest and fastest-moving part of AI. I teach it with projects I've built myself, including agents that query databases and search the web.

    Ask about agentic AI training

    Screenshot: an AI agent project3:2

  • AI mentoring, one on one

    If you want a personal AI mentor, I work with you over several weeks. It suits career switchers, students and researchers. We set a learning roadmap, pick a project, and meet regularly so you keep moving. I made this switch myself, from lab research to AI, so I can tell you what to learn first and what to skip.

  • Freelance AI trainer for hire

    As a freelance AI trainer, I take on single sessions, short courses and custom training programmes. Tell me your audience and goal, and I'll design the content around it.

Who and where

Who I train, and where

Who it's for:

  • Students and fresh graduates who want an AI skill that helps them get hired
  • Career switchers, especially people from biology, commerce, arts and other non-tech fields
  • Working professionals and business owners who want to use AI in daily work
  • College faculty and researchers who want to bring AI into teaching and research
  • Companies and teams with no in-house AI expertise

In person across Kerala, online across India

I'm based in Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram), Kerala, and I take sessions in person across Kerala. If you're elsewhere, I teach online, for colleges, companies and learners in any city.

Cities I cover in person:

  • Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram)
  • Kochi and Ernakulam
  • Kollam
  • Alappuzha
  • Thrissur
  • Kozhikode

Other places in Kerala on request.
