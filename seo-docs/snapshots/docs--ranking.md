### Google Search ranking hub

The Google Search ranking hub is your central place to learn how Google ranks search results and to keep up with changes to our ranking systems.

## Our principles for ranking results

We rank all types of results based on their usefulness to the user's query. We don't design our ranking systems or AI models to favor any specific site's interests, and we [never accept payment](https://www.google.com/search/howsearchworks/our-approach/honest-results/) to rank web pages higher in organic search results. Every part of our system is guided by three principles.

[Read our ranking principles](/search/docs/ranking/principles-for-ranking-results)

- #### Users come first
- #### Consistent systems across all types of results
- #### Rigorous testing

## Stay informed and submit feedback

### [Stay informed on upcoming changes](/search/docs/ranking/stay-informed)

To give site owners predictability and time to prepare, we publish advance notice of actionable policy changes and significant ranking updates on the Google Search Status Dashboard.

[Stay informed](/search/docs/ranking/stay-informed)

### [Submit feedback on ranking](/search/docs/ranking/submit-feedback)

We provide various mechanisms for publishers to submit feedback about ranking updates in Google Search or request dispute resolution.

[Submit ranking feedback](/search/docs/ranking/submit-feedback)

### [Learn how ranking works](https://www.google.com/search/howsearchworks/how-search-works/ranking-results/)

Our automated ranking systems look at many factors and signals to sort through hundreds of billions of web pages and find the most relevant, useful results for each search.

[Learn how we rank results](https://www.google.com/search/howsearchworks/how-search-works/ranking-results/)

## Troubleshoot ranking changes

If your site's performance shifts and you suspect a ranking or policy change is the cause, these resources can help you understand and troubleshoot the issue.

### [Debug traffic drops](/search/docs/monitor-debug/debugging-search-traffic-drops)

Follow our step-by-step diagnostic guide to analyze Search Console traffic changes after an update completes.

Ask questions and get help from other site owners, publishers, and Google Product Experts.
