# Regional differences in Search experience

Google is evolving the search results page for certain types of queries to provide new opportunities for Vertical Search Services (VSS), Comparison Shopping Services (CSS), direct suppliers, and content providers.

To find the features relevant to your website, select where your company is based and the query types that are most relevant to your business:

| Feature | Region | Queries |

[image: Aggregator unit in Google Search]

**Aggregator unit**

A unit that provides visibility for ecosystem participants (aggregators) on the search results page.

[Learn about the aggregator unit](/search/docs/appearance/aggregator-unit)

EEA

Hotels, flights, ground transportation, local businesses, things to do, and products

[image: Supplier unit in Google Search]

**Supplier unit**

A unit that provides visibility for ecosystem participants (direct suppliers) on the search results page.

[Learn about the supplier unit](/search/docs/appearance/supplier-unit)

EEA

Hotels, flights, ground transportation, local businesses, things to do, and products

[image: Ecosystem carousel in Google Search]

**Ecosystem carousel**

A carousel that may appear alongside answers that require data visualizations and interactivity with data (for example, stock charts, weather information, sports scores).

[Learn about the ecosystem carousel](/search/docs/appearance/ecosystem-carousel)

EEA

Weather, sports, finance, and translate

[image: Job sites in Google Search]

**Job sites features**

A dedicated "Job sites" carousel and refinement chip that includes links to aggregator sites from across the web with the option to see more.

[Learn about the job sites features](/search/docs/appearance/job-sites)

EEA

Jobs

[image: Places sites in Google Search]

**Places sites features**

A dedicated "Places sites" carousel and refinement chip that includes links to aggregator sites from across the web with the option to see more.

[Learn about the places sites features](/search/docs/appearance/places-sites)

Türkiye

Hotels and local businesses

[image: South African badge on a Google Search result] [image: South African refinement chip in Google Search]

**South Africa badge and refinement chip**

A badge and refinement chip are available for businesses based in South Africa for specific query types.

[Learn about the South Africa features](/search/docs/appearance/south-africa-features)

South Africa

Travel, products, car hire, food delivery, and ground transportation

[image: Structured data carousels in Google Search]

**Structured data carousels**

A list-like rich result that people can scroll horizontally to see more entities from a given site (also known as a host carousel). To be eligible, your site must implement structured data markup.

[Learn about the carousel](/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/carousels-beta)

EEA

Hotels, local businesses, things to do, products, ground transportation, flights, and vacation rentals

| South Africa | Hotels, things to do, flights, products, food delivery, car hire, vacation rentals, and ground transportation |
| Türkiye | Hotels, local businesses, and vacation rentals |
