# How to stay informed on Google Search ranking updates

Google regularly updates its ranking systems and policies to keep Search helpful and reliable for everyone. This page explains when we pre-announce changes, our advance notice timelines, and where to track confirmed updates.

## When we give notice

When an update introduces actionable policy requirements or may cause broad ranking shifts for site owners, we provide notice based on the type of change. Additionally, for changes where UK users may experience a significant ranking shift, we provide advance notice to site owners in accordance with [CMA's fair ranking conduct requirements for Google](https://www.gov.uk/find-digital-markets-measures/google-search-fair-ranking-conduct-requirement).

Here are some examples of what changes would result in a notice:

- **Actionable policy changes:** Updates to our [Search content policies](/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies), technical guidelines, or eligibility criteria where site owners may need to adjust their site, content practices, or markup to remain compliant.
- **Significant ranking changes:** Broad algorithmic updates (such as [core updates](/search/docs/appearance/core-updates)) that may produce noticeable ranking shifts across many queries and sites.

## Track confirmed updates and pre-announcements

We publish pre-announcements, rollout start dates, and completion notices on the [Search Status Dashboard](https://status.search.google.com/products/rGHU1u87FJnkP6W2GwMi/history). You can also subscribe to automatic updates through the [Atom / RSS feed](https://status.search.google.com/en/feed.atom).

We also post about major updates on the [Google Search Central Blog](/search/blog) and on our [documentation changelog page](/search/updates), which both have RSS feeds that you can subscribe to and receive updates as they happen.

## What to do when seeing issues after an update

Wait until an update is marked complete on the Search Status Dashboard (plus at least a week of post-rollout data) before evaluating traffic changes in Search Console:

- [Debug drops in Search traffic](/search/docs/monitor-debug/debugging-search-traffic-drops) to analyze performance changes.
- [Submit feedback about ranking changes](/search/docs/ranking/submit-feedback).
