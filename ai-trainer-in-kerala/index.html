Why a biologist makes a good AI trainer

I spent about seven years in research, at IISER Thiruvananthapuram, ICAR's Central Institute of Brackishwater Aquaculture in Chennai, and Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute (SCTIMST), where I worked for over four years as a Senior Research Fellow. I've published two book chapters and three research articles. I qualified the UGC-NET for Assistant Professor in Life Sciences in 2019.

In 2023 I moved into data science, then into generative AI. I learned it the hard way: reading, building, breaking things, rebuilding. That's why I teach it the way I do. I explain one idea at a time, I show it working, and I make sure you build something yourself before the session ends.

What I've built, which is what I teach from

I'm a Gen AI Engineer at Blackbox Learning, and I've built 18 AI projects of my own. A few of them:

  • Chatbots that answer questions from your own PDFs and documents (RAG)
  • AI agents that use tools, search, and query databases in plain English
  • Fine-tuned language models, and apps deployed on AWS
  • Computer vision work, including brain tumour detection from MRI scans

I don't teach what I've only read about. Everything in my sessions is something I've built.

Teaching experience

I've been teaching since 2013, starting with biology and school-level mathematics. Today I teach machines how to behave like humans, and teach humans how to use, and build, machines that behave like humans.

I started teaching AI in February 2024. I created workshops and learning material, taught statistics, Python, machine learning and generative AI, and mentored students through placements. I now train students, working professionals and teams in generative AI and agentic AI.

I've trained and mentored more than 100 students and professionals, through workshops, courses and one-on-one mentoring.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is the best AI trainer in Kerala for beginners?

The best fit is someone who remembers being a beginner. I'm a Trivandrum-based AI trainer who started with no tech background and taught myself AI. I explain things in plain language, in English or Malayalam, and every session ends with something you've built.

Do I need coding or a technical background to join your AI training?

No. Most of my learners are not technical. We start with how AI works in simple terms and with tools you can use right away. Coding is added only if your goal needs it.

Can you train my company's team as a corporate AI trainer?

Yes. I design corporate AI training around your team's real tasks, such as documents, reports, customer messages and data. Tell me the team size and the work they do, and I'll propose a plan.

Do you run AI workshops for colleges and schools?

Yes. As an AI workshop trainer, I run half-day and full-day workshops for students and faculty, in person in Kerala or online anywhere in India.

What is the difference between generative AI and agentic AI training?

Generative AI training covers tools that create text, images and answers from a prompt, like ChatGPT. Agentic AI training goes a step further: AI that plans, uses tools and completes multi-step tasks on its own. I teach both, starting with generative AI.

Are you available as a freelance AI trainer outside Kerala?

Yes. I take freelance training work across India, mostly online. For in-person sessions outside Kerala, we can discuss travel.

Do you offer one-on-one AI mentoring?

Yes. As an AI mentor, I work with career switchers, students and researchers over several weeks: a roadmap, a real project, and regular check-ins.

How do I book you?

Send a message on WhatsApp or email with your city, group size and goal. I reply with what I'd recommend and how we could work together.

What are your fees for AI training?

Fees depend on the audience, group size, number of days, and whether the session is online or in person. Message me with those details and I'll send you a quote.
