How it works

Several weeks, one clear direction

  1. A learning roadmap

    We set it together, so you know what to learn first and what to skip.

  2. A real project

    We pick a project, and you build it. You learn AI by making something that works.

  3. Regular check-ins

    We meet regularly so you keep moving.

Why me

I made the same switch

I didn't start in tech. I started in a biology lab, working on stem cells, vaccines and 3D bioprinted skin. I taught myself AI from scratch, built my own projects, and then started teaching it.

In 2023 I moved into data science, then into generative AI. I learned it the hard way: reading, building, breaking things, rebuilding. That's why I teach it the way I do. I explain one idea at a time, I show it working, and I make sure you build something yourself.

At Dexpro Innovations I mentored students through placements. I've trained and mentored more than 100 students and professionals.

FAQ

Questions about mentoring

Do you offer one-on-one AI mentoring?

Yes. As an AI mentor, I work with career switchers, students and researchers over several weeks: a roadmap, a real project, and regular check-ins.

Do I need coding or a technical background to join your AI training?

No. Most of my learners are not technical. We start with how AI works in simple terms and with tools you can use right away. Coding is added only if your goal needs it.

How do I book you?

Send a message on WhatsApp or email with your city, group size and goal. I reply with what I'd recommend and how we could work together.

What are your fees for AI training?

Fees depend on the audience, group size, number of days, and whether the session is online or in person. Message me with those details and I'll send you a quote.
